01 Summary

Next steps

05 Recommendations

Our recommendations from this period. Nothing changes in your accounts until you've agreed.

Meta creative
Add new influencer ads to retargeting before peak season
One ad, "Influencer. Flex Media v2", delivered 35 of Meta's 43 sales. Retargeting frequency is 3.9, so the same people see these ads about four times a month. New influencer variations spread the load so results don't depend on a single ad.
Meta prospecting
Test a sales-focused prospecting campaign
The prospecting campaign spent $593.13 with no tracked sales. It did bring 4,497 link clicks at $0.13 each and 33 add-to-carts, which feed the retargeting audience. We recommend keeping it and testing a sales-optimised version alongside it.
Performance Max
Hold Performance Max budget and review its product focus
Return fell to 3.60x from 5.83x, and cost per sale rose to $92.49 from $64.97. Shopping improved to 5.24x over the same period. Both campaigns had fewer than 30 sales, so we'd hold budgets steady while we review, rather than move money yet.
Search exclusions
Approve the 14 proposed search exclusions
We.ve prepared a short list of searches for products you don't sell and budget retailers (for example, tankini and kmart). Approving them keeps spend on shoppers looking for your range as search volume grows.
Google search
Grow sales from people who don't know the brand yet
Searches for "saint somebody" and its variations drove 14.6 of the 26.5 sales we can see at search level. Broad searches like "plus size swimwear" cost $102.13 for 1 sale. We'll test product and audience changes that lift these non-brand searches.
Peak season
Agree budgets for the October to February swim season
The final week returned 6.33x, close to the 6.46x of mid-August. With results recovering, now is the time to set peak-season budgets and targets together.
