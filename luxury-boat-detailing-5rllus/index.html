Next steps

04 Recommendations

Our recommendations from this period. Nothing changes in your account until you've agreed.

Quote requests
Back the strongest lead ad
The original “Get A Quote” ad produced 11 of the 14 requests at $20.48 each. Version 2 is at $39.12 from 3 requests, so we recommend giving more of the budget to the original.
Video
Favour the shorter videos
“Video Levels” and “Employ the best” bring visitors in at $0.40 and $0.41. The longer video costs $1.05 per visit, so we'd move its budget to the shorter cuts.
Enquiries
Tell us which requests become jobs
Meta counts a completed quote form, not a booked job. Letting us know which enquiries turn into work means we can steer budget towards the people who actually book.
